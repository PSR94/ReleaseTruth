# Code of Conduct

ReleaseTruth follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

We are committed to a harassment-free, inclusive community. Be respectful, assume technical disagreement is normal, critique ideas rather than people, and avoid behavior that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the repository's security/contact channels. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this code of conduct.

The full Contributor Covenant 2.1 text is available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
